The situation

American Tower needed to cut energy cost and improve power reliability across its national tower portfolio. The programme rolled out Smart Grid Systems, Grid Retrofits and Grid as a Service across 1,000 telecoms sites, alongside lithium-ion and lithium iron phosphate battery storage with solar infrastructure across 1,000+ sites.

My approach

I co-led the rollout using a gated governance model with Agile planning inside each phase. Every phase passed a stage gate with structured reporting before the next began, which gave leadership a clear view of progress and kept site work aligned with budget and design standards.

The hard part

Fifteen third-party suppliers were delivering at the same time, often competing for the same crews, equipment and site access windows. I negotiated resource prioritisation across all fifteen so the highest-value sites moved first and no supplier was left blocking another.

The outcome

  • 30% improvement in energy efficiency
  • 22% reduction in operating costs
  • 1,000+ sites with improved storage reliability
  • 99.5% tower uptime maintained across the portfolio

Read the case study

The situation

Scenic International was modernising its core business systems across several sites: NetSuite ERP, Boomi and MuleSoft integrations, Azure, Microsoft 365, telephony and CRM. The business was ISO 27001 certified, so the programme had to change how data moved between systems without weakening its security posture or putting certification at risk. The portfolio was worth over £500K and ran as five to seven workstreams at once.

My approach

I ran the portfolio under PRINCE2 with hybrid Agile delivery inside each workstream, leading cross-functional teams of 10 to 15. I treated ISO 27001 compliance as a delivery requirement, not a check after go-live: it sat in each workstream’s acceptance criteria, and critical risks were tracked centrally and reviewed before any release.

The hard part

Every new integration creates a new data flow, and every data flow is new risk surface. With several workstreams moving in parallel, critical risks surfaced at the same time and competed for the same people. Rather than accept open critical risks into live service, I prioritised closing them before launch, and resolved more than 85% of them pre-launch.

The outcome

  • 100% ISO 27001 compliance maintained across multi-site environments
  • 85%+ of critical risks resolved before launch
  • 98% on-time delivery across concurrent workstreams
  • 30% improvement in operational efficiency

The situation

American Tower was delivering 1,500+ tower upgrades and 200+ new site builds across a nationally distributed estate, with 40+ projects active at once. Work was slowed by rework, inconsistent vendor performance and slow, manual reporting to senior leadership.

My approach

I helped build PMO governance around control gates that each piece of work had to pass, and standardised how schedules, resources and stakeholder reporting were managed. I built Power BI and Smartsheet dashboards that brought data from all 40+ projects into a single real-time view of portfolio health for the COO.

The hard part

Engineering and EPC partners were meeting only 82% of their SLAs, which drove delays and rework. I introduced structured vendor performance appraisals with regular feedback loops, so each partner knew exactly where they stood and what to fix.

The outcome

  • 82% → 94% vendor SLA compliance
  • 25% fewer rework cycles
  • 18% increase in delivery efficiency
  • 12% faster site readiness
  • 35% less time spent on PMO reporting
